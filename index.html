The pickers

Overwatch Hero Picker

owheropicker.com

Tick the heroes you're happy to play — or a whole role — and let it choose. Great for warming up, for climbing out of a one-trick rut, or for running your own Mystery Heroes.

  • ▸ Filter by hero or by role — Tank, Damage, Support
  • ▸ Squad Generator for full team compositions
  • ▸ Randomize perks along with the hero
  • ▸ Save presets and share a roll with a link
Open the picker Source on GitHub

Valorant Agent Picker

valorantpicker.com

Same idea, for Valorant. Narrow the pool to the agents you know — or the ones you're trying to learn — and get one at random instead of staring at the agent select clock.

  • ▸ Filter by agent or by role — Duelist, Controller, Initiator, Sentinel
  • ▸ Squad Generator for a full five-stack
  • ▸ Save presets for your comfort picks
  • ▸ Share a roll or a squad with a link

Both sites are free, and stay that way

Both pickers are free to use and run without ads. There is no paid tier and nothing is held back, so donations are the only way to support them.

They also need keeping up to date: every new hero and agent means new art, new icons and a roster update, usually within days of release. Donations pay for the hosting and for that ongoing work.
